*📊 Credit Risk Analysis Dashboard*



*📌 Project Overview*



*This project analyzes a loan default dataset to identify the key factors influencing borrower default risk. Using MySQL for data exploration and Power BI for visualization, the dashboard provides actionable insights into borrower demographics, financial characteristics, and loan performance to support better credit risk assessment.*



*🎯 Business Problem*



*Financial institutions need to accurately identify borrowers who are more likely to default on their loans. Ineffective credit risk assessment can lead to financial losses and poor lending decisions.*



*This project aims to:*



*Analyze borrower demographics and financial profiles.*

*Identify factors associated with loan default.*

*Provide interactive dashboards to support credit risk analysis.*

*Generate business insights that assist lending decisions.*

*💡 Business Insights*



*The overall loan default rate is 11.61%, indicating that nearly 1 in 9 borrowers defaulted on their loans.*

*Borrowers with poor credit scores showed the highest default rates, highlighting credit score as a key risk indicator.*

*Borrowers who defaulted had a lower average income than non-defaulters, suggesting income influences repayment capacity.*

*Defaulters were associated with higher average interest rates, indicating increased borrowing costs may contribute to repayment challenges.*

*Loan amount alone was not a strong predictor of default, as non-defaulters received higher average loan amounts.*

*Employment type and loan purpose exhibited different default patterns, suggesting these characteristics should be considered during credit risk assessment.*



*📌 Business Recommendations*



*Prioritize credit score and income during the loan approval process.*

*Implement enhanced risk assessment for borrowers with poor credit histories.*

*Combine demographic and financial indicators for more accurate credit evaluations.*

*Monitor high-risk borrower segments to reduce future loan defaults.*

*Develop predictive models to support data-driven lending decisions.*
